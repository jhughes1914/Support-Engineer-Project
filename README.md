# Support Engineer Project

## Objective

The Support Engineer project aimed as a mock Support Engineer at Delta Air Lines, focusing on the Fly Delta mobile app. The app allows customers to book flights, check in, view flight status, access boarding passes, track luggage, and manage their SkyMiles accounts. Within the project, Delta has been receiving an increasing number of support tickets related to various issues within the app. The job was to analyzed tickets, identify patterns, and propose solutions. Objective of the project is to provide thoughtful, fleshed-out responses, tact, and great use of language.

1. _Tasks_:
- Analyze the dataset and identify the top 3 most common issues.  
- Create a visual representation (e.g., chart or graph) showing the distribution of tickets by category.  
- Identify any patterns in the timing of tickets (e.g., do certain issues occur more frequently at specific times of day or days of the week?).  
- Propose a method to categorize and prioritize these tickets more effectively. 
- In your opinion, what is the highest priority item that needs to be fixed? Explain your reasoning.  
- What other pieces of information can we collect from customers to help identify the root cause of the issues?


2. _Provide Solution Proposal_: (Based on analysis, choose one of the top issues thats identified)  
- Write a detailed plan on how to address this issue, including: 
  - Root cause analysis  
  - Short-term solution to mitigate the problem  
  - Long-term solution to prevent the issue from recurring 
- Draft an email to the development team explaining the issue and your proposed solution. Remember to be clear, concise, and professional.  
- Create a simple FAQ or troubleshooting guide that could be sent to users experiencing this issue.


3. _Costumer Communication_: (Choosing a complex ticket from the dataset and writing two responses)
- A technical explanation of the issue and its solution, as you would communicate it to the development team.  
- A simplified explanation of the same issue and solution, as you would communicate it to a non-technical user. 

### Skills Learned
- Working with Excel spreadsheet: reviewing and analyzing a series of dataset and configuring into a table for analyzing, filtering categories and data, and creating graph for visual representation.
- DevOps skills identifying root causes and providing short and long term solutions to mitigate and prevent issues from occuring.
- Communication skills that would convey to development teams to identify and ways to resolve issues, and relaying current situations and solutions to non-technical users.

### Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel to create a table and label datasets and the option to filter dataset.
